# Third-party material

- **Metropolis** and **Atkinson Hyperlegible** fonts in `assets/fonts/` are carried over from the
  author's earlier app, Resonant. Both are open-licensed fonts (SIL Open Font License); confirm the licence
  text at the source before redistributing. Metropolis came from
  <https://github.com/fontsource/font-files/tree/main/fonts/other/metropolis/files>; Atkinson Hyperlegible is
  from the Braille Institute.
- **Meta Model API** (Muse Spark 1.3, SAM 3.1) is used under Meta's terms: <https://dev.meta.ai>.
- Mask format parsing uses `@meta-sam/parser` (see `backend/package.json`).
- The one frame of SAM output used as a test fixture (`backend/test/helpers.js`) is copied from Meta's SAM documentation.
